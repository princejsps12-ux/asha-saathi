The problem · field research

Paperwork eats the Mitanin's eveningWe spoke to 3 Mitanin workers in Gharghoda block, Raigarh, Chhattisgarh.

What they told us

  • 3–4 registers per pregnant woman
  • 1–2 hours of paperwork every day, mostly in the evening or at night
  • The same data written twice: in the register and again on the phone
  • Network often fails, so entry is delayed

The solution · core voice flow

Speak once. ASHA Saathi does the register.एक बार बोलिए — रजिस्टर अपने-आप भरेगा।

🎤
Speak the visitHindi, Hinglish or English, at your own pace
📋
Fields filledWoman, month, BP, Hb, symptoms, IFA, Td, next visit
🔊
Read back aloudIn Hindi (or English) — she hears what will be saved
✅
“हाँ” → savedSay yes, or correct by voice: “बीपी 150 बाय 100”
🚨
Danger alertHigh BP, bleeding, reduced movement… with the reason
📅
Due list updatesDue tomorrow, high-risk list, visit history
Zero typing for the whole visit — voice in, voice back, “हाँ” to save. Big buttons as a tap fallback.

Understands Hindi number words (“एक सौ चालीस बटा नब्बे”), BP said many ways (“140 by 90”, “ऊपर 140 नीचे 90”), dates (“कल”, “परसों”, “अगले हफ्ते”, “15 तारीख”) and Chhattisgarhi / Bhojpuri words for common symptoms.

Safety and trust

It asks instead of guessingगलत रिकॉर्ड से बेहतर है दोबारा पूछना

  • Read-back before save — nothing is saved until she hears it and says “हाँ”.
  • “BP समझ नहीं आया — दोबारा बोलिए / BP not clear, say again” instead of guessing a mis-heard number.
  • Never assumes “normal” — unclear baby movement is asked again; an unknown name asks “Add as new woman?”.
  • Decision support only — refers to a 24-hour PHC or FRU; it does not diagnose.

Every danger rule is cited (document + page), and every alert says why

  • MoHFW ANC & Skilled Attendance at Birth guideline, 2010
  • NHSRC CHO training manual on care during pregnancy, 2021
  • Mother and Child Protection (MCP) Card, 2018
  • Anemia Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan guidelines, 2026
Sources: MoHFW “Guidelines for Antenatal Care and Skilled Attendance at Birth by ANMs/LHVs/SNs” (2010) · NHSRC “Training Manual on Care During Pregnancy and Child Birth for CHO at AB-HWC” (2021) · MoHFW & MoWCD MCP Card (2018) · MoHFW “Anemia Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan Operational Guidelines” (June 2026). What it depends on — and what if it gets costly or stops

No paid AI and no API keys in the core flowकोई API की नहीं — पैसे वाली सेवा बंद हो तो भी ऐप चलता रहे

DependencyCostIf it gets costly or stops working
Browser speech recognition (built into Chrome)Free · needs internetType-in box, plus an offline save queue: visits are kept on the phone and synced when the network returns
Our rule-based parser + danger rulesFree · our codeRuns fully on our server — no paid AI, no keys. Nothing to switch off
Read-back (browser text-to-speech)Free · on deviceThe same summary is always shown as text, with big हाँ / नहीं buttons
Optional: Sarvam speech-to-text, Gemini extractionPaid · optionalOff by default. If costly, slow or down: automatic fallback to the built-in parser — the visit still works
Hosting: Render free tier (Docker)FreeOne docker compose up runs it on any Docker host — including state NIC servers
